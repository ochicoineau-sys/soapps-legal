2. What the app does

It helps you find foundation shades in other brands that are close to a shade you already wear, or close to a depth and undertone you tell it about. It also has two camera shortcuts: reading a product label, and estimating your depth from a selfie. It runs entirely on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in.

4. Everything here is an estimate. This matters more than anything else in these terms.

The app compares shades using the depth and undertone that brands publish, refined by a published swatch colour. It does not measure anything.

  • Matches are estimates, not measurements. No laboratory colour measurement is involved. The app says so, next to the results.
  • Swatch colours are illustrative. They come from the shade data as published; they are not measurements of the product, and screens differ anyway.
  • The selfie estimate estimates depth only, and approximately. Light, camera and screen all affect it. It is a starting point you are invited to correct.
  • The catalogue is a dated snapshot. Shades are added, reformulated and discontinued. The app tells you when its data was obtained.

Always try a shade on your skin, in daylight, before buying. Nothing the app shows is a substitute for that.
