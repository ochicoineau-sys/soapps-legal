Privacy Policy — VitaFolio

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Terms of use

VitaFolio keeps and organises your own health documents. Your record is hosted in the European Union. We do not sell your data, we show no advertising and use no trackers, and your data is not used to train AI models. You can export or delete everything from the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

VitaFolio is operated by Olivier Chicoineau, an individual (not a company), Avenida 327, 6, 08860 Castelldefels, Spain. He is the controller of your personal data within the meaning of Article 4(7) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — “we” in this policy.

Privacy and data-protection contact: o.chicoineau@gmail.com.

No data protection officer has been designated, as none is required at our current scale (Article 37 GDPR); write to the controller directly.

2. What VitaFolio is

VitaFolio is a personal health record, available as an Android app, an iPhone app (currently in beta) and a web app. It keeps your health documents, reads the values they contain when you ask it to, and prepares summaries you can share with a doctor. It organises and displays information: it gives no diagnosis, no treatment advice and no risk assessment.

3. The data we process

  • Account: your e-mail address. You sign in with a 6-digit code (and a link) that Supabase Auth sends to that address; there is no password.
  • Profile (optional): name, year of birth, sex, countries, language, units, blood type, emergency contact (name and phone number), smoking, alcohol and physical activity.
  • Health data (a special category of data, Article 9 GDPR): documents and their original files (lab reports, imaging, letters, discharge summaries, prescriptions, vaccination records and others), lab results, conditions, past illnesses, surgeries, hospital stays, allergies, medicines and reminders (with your “Taken” / “Skipped” answers), vaccinations, family history, life events (for example travel, diet, pregnancy or illness), measurements (blood pressure, weight, resting heart rate, blood glucose) and the summaries you share.
  • Values from Health Connect or Apple Health, only if you connect them (section 8).
  • Subscription: plan, renewal date, store and subscription events (section 12).
  • Share history: the date and language of each view of a summary link (section 10).
  • Web reminders (optional): your browser’s push subscription and a 14-day queue of reminder times (section 11).
  • Usage counters: the number of AI calls and tokens per feature and per date, used to apply fair-use limits. They contain no content.
  • Technical logs: our server functions log only the function name, the status, the duration and the error type — never content or health data. Our hosts keep their own technical logs (for example IP addresses) for security, for a limited period.

Your e-mail address is needed to create an account; everything else is optional and you decide what to add. The data come from you, from Health Connect or Apple Health if you connect them, and, for a subscription, from Google Play.

4. Why we use your data, and on what legal basis

  • Providing your account and the service (sign-in, storing and showing your record, summaries, sharing links, reminders, fair-use limits, and your subscription and billing records): performance of our contract with you — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Health data: your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give when you add health information to your record after being informed by this policy. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting that information or your account; this does not affect earlier processing.
  • Health Connect / Apple Health: the consent you give in the system permission screen, which you can withdraw there at any time.
  • Records of other people (section 9): the explicit consent of the person concerned, or of their parent or legal guardian, as declared by the account holder — Article 9(2)(a) GDPR.
  • Security logs: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models.

5. Service providers that process your data

The following providers process data on our behalf (processors). Google Play takes subscription payments under its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase — account, sign-in e-mails, database, file storage and server functions. Our project is hosted in the European Union: Frankfurt, Germany (AWS eu-central-1). It holds everything stored in your account. Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest is provided by our host.
  • Anthropic (United States) — reads documents and translates your own free text, only through our server functions. Details below.
  • RevenueCat (United States) — manages the subscription status. It receives from us only a random account identifier (no health data, name or e-mail address), plus the purchase information from Google Play; like any purchase service, it also sees technical data such as the IP address.
  • Google Play — takes the payment for subscriptions, under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • Vercel — serves the web version of the app. It receives your IP address and standard request data, only to deliver the pages.
  • Browser push services (Google, Mozilla or Apple, depending on your browser) — only if you turn on reminders in the installed web app; they deliver the notifications (section 11).
  • GitHub (United States) — hosts these policy pages (GitHub Pages) and nothing else; it receives your IP address when you open them.

What is sent to Anthropic, and what is not

  • Lab reports (PDF or photo), to read the results.
  • Photos of medicine boxes, prescriptions and health-booklet pages, to transcribe them.
  • Any file added with “+” that your device cannot classify by itself, to classify it.
  • For “Questions to ask your doctor”: facts about the one result concerned.
  • Your own free text (reason for the visit, notes, event labels, questions you rewrote), to translate it into the app’s five languages when you share a summary or add a QR code to the emergency card.

Never sent to Anthropic: values from Health Connect or Apple Health, your e-mail address, your name. Documents are sent whole: the model is instructed not to return names, identifiers or addresses found in them, but the document itself may contain them.

Anthropic processes these data under its commercial terms and Data Processing Addendum, which include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Under those terms, Anthropic does not use API inputs or outputs to train its models and keeps them only for a limited period set by its retention policy.

After reading, the original file of a lab report stays in your record until you delete it. Photos of medicine boxes, prescription images and health-booklet pages are not kept unless you tick “keep”; the prescriber’s name is kept only if you choose so.

6. Transfers outside the European Union

Your record is stored in the EU. Anthropic and RevenueCat are based in the United States; transfers to them are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Article 46(2)(c) GDPR) included in their data-processing terms. You can ask us for information about these safeguards, or a copy of them.

7. What stays on your device

  • Barcodes and QR codes, including the code of French digital prescriptions, are decoded on your device.
  • The text of PDF files is read on your device to sort them.
  • Photo metadata (EXIF, which can include a location) is removed on your device before a photo is sent.
  • The consultation brief (doctor summary) is put together on your device from your record; only your own free text is sent for translation (section 5).
  • The summary PDF, the emergency card and the export ZIP file are built on your device. On the phone, the app writes the export, PDF or image it creates to its own temporary cache to hand it to the share sheet; the system may keep that copy until it clears the cache, and uninstalling the app removes it.
  • The “sample data” demo record is fictional and stays on your device.

8. Health Connect and Apple Health

  • Only with your permission, VitaFolio reads four types of data from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone): blood pressure, weight, resting heart rate and blood glucose. It never writes to them.
  • The first connection reads up to the last 12 months; after that, VitaFolio reads new values automatically at most every six hours when you open the app, or when you tap “Bring in new values”. Values go only into the account holder’s own record, never into another person’s.
  • Each value is stored in your record with the name of the app or device that recorded it and the store’s record identifier (to avoid importing a value twice).
  • These values are not sent to Anthropic, to RevenueCat or to any advertiser. A summary you choose to share can include statistics (number of readings, period, latest value, 90-day mean) of all your self-measured values, typed or imported.
  • Disconnecting stops new imports; values already imported stay in your record until you delete them.
  • Our use of these data complies with the Health Connect and Apple HealthKit rules, including the Health Connect Limited Use requirements: they are not used for advertising, not sold, and not used for anything other than showing them in your record and in the summaries you choose to share.

9. Records of other people

You can keep records for other people — children, parents, a partner or others — after declaring that you are entitled to do so, as their parent or legal guardian or with their consent. You are responsible for that entitlement. These people have the same rights as you (section 17): they can exercise them through the account holder or by writing to us.

10. Sharing a summary, and the emergency card

  • A summary link is valid for 24 hours, and you can revoke it at any time. Anyone who has the link can open the summary while it is valid, without an account.
  • Our server keeps a hash of the link (never the link itself) and a copy of the summary until the link expires or is revoked. Revoking erases that copy at once; an expired link opens nothing and its copy is deleted. Each view is recorded (date and language — no IP address).
  • If you tick “Include my reminder log (last 4 weeks)”, the summary also shows, for each medicine, how many of the scheduled reminders you marked “Taken” over the last 4 weeks.
  • The emergency card is an image made on your device, which you share yourself. Once shared, copies are outside VitaFolio. If you add a QR code to the card, the QR code opens a 24-hour summary link, stored and handled exactly like the links above (its free text is translated as described in section 5).

11. Reminders

  • In the iPhone and Android apps, reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
  • In the installed web app, if you turn them on, reminders use Web Push: our server stores your browser’s push subscription and a 14-day queue of reminder times, and the notification is delivered by your browser’s push service (Google, Mozilla or Apple). Its text is generic unless you choose to show the medicine’s name.

12. Subscriptions

Subscriptions (VitaFolio Plus) are currently sold only in the Android app and paid through Google Play; Google’s privacy policy covers the payment. RevenueCat manages the subscription status; it receives from us only a random account identifier (no health data, name or e-mail address), plus the purchase information from Google Play, and like any purchase service it also sees technical data such as the IP address. We store your plan, renewal date, store and subscription events.

13. Cookies and local storage

The app sets no cookies and uses no advertising identifier, no analytics or crash-reporting tool and no tracker. Fonts and code are served from our own origin. The app keeps your sign-in session and your settings (language, units, selected person, reminder and Health Connect status, reminder snoozes, chart display options and the sample-data state) in your device’s local storage; nothing from your record is kept there, except the sample record if you open it. This storage is strictly necessary to provide the service you ask for (ePrivacy rules). The Android app is excluded from Android’s cloud backup.

14. How long we keep data, and deletion

  • We keep your data while your account exists, including usage counters, subscription events and share history. You can delete any item in the app at any time.
  • Deleting your account (Settings › Delete my account and all data) deletes all your files first, then your account and every record linked to it. If a file cannot be deleted, the account is not deleted and the app asks you to try again.
  • Deleted data can remain in our host’s encrypted backups for a limited period before being overwritten.
  • Records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for billing and tax obligations follow their own policies.
  • Exports, PDFs and images you have saved or shared outside the app, and the temporary cache copy described in section 7, are not affected.

15. Export

Settings › Export all my data gives you a ZIP file with data.json (profile, persons, list of documents, results, profile entries, life events, medicines, reminder answers, measurements, share history) and your original files (right to data portability, Article 20 GDPR).

16. Age

VitaFolio is intended for adults (18 and over). Records of children are held by a parent or legal guardian.

17. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time (Articles 7(3) and 15 to 21 GDPR). Most of them can be exercised directly in the app: edit, delete, export. Otherwise, write to o.chicoineau@gmail.com; we answer within one month (Article 12(3) GDPR).

18. Complaints

You can lodge a complaint with the Spanish data-protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD, www.aepd.es), or with the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live, where you work or where the alleged infringement took place (Article 77 GDPR).

19. No automated decisions

VitaFolio takes no decision based solely on automated processing that has legal or similarly significant effects on you (Article 22 GDPR): the app shows information; it decides nothing.

20. Not medical advice

VitaFolio organises and displays your information. It gives no diagnosis, no treatment advice and no risk assessment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional; in an emergency, call 112.

21. Changes to this policy

We announce material changes in the app before they apply. The date at the top of this page shows the current version.
